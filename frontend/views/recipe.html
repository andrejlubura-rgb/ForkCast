Spaghetti Carbonara

Pasta TheMealDB

A Roman classic with pancetta, eggs and Parmesan, ready in about 25 minutes.

Prep: 25 min Servings: 2
Edit

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti 200 g
  • Pancetta 100 g
  • Eggs 2
  • Parmesan 50 g
  • Black pepper 1 tsp
  • Salt 1 pinch

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Fry the pancetta in a dry pan over medium heat until crisp.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the grated Parmesan and plenty of black pepper.
  4. Drain the pasta, keeping a little cooking water, and toss it with the pancetta.
  5. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the egg mixture with a splash of pasta water and serve.
