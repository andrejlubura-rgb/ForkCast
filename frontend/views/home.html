Frequently asked questions

Short answers about saving recipes, filling your week and building the shopping list.

Discover recipes

From two places. You can write your own recipes and import meals from TheMealDB on the Discover page. Imported meals stay in your collection, so you can plan them again without searching for them twice.

Yes. Recipes, meal plans and shopping lists belong to your account, so log in first. Every user plans their own week, and an admin also manages the categories and users.

Each slot holds one meal. A day has breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack slots, so plan another meal in the same slot to swap it. One meal per slot is what keeps the shopping list accurate.

ForkCast collects the ingredients of every planned meal and combines their amounts, so you shop once for the whole week instead of once per recipe. Change a meal in the planner and the list follows.

You can edit the recipes you created, and an admin can edit any recipe. A recipe used in a plan cannot be deleted until it is removed from that plan, so the shopping list never loses an ingredient.
